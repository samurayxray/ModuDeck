## Half Life import

The root `BOM.csv` contains the same English parts list. Connect `https://github.com/samurayxray/ModuDeck` to the Half Life project, then try **Import from repo**. The importer has not been tested; it may require different columns or complete vendor and price fields. If it rejects the draft, use this table to add the parts manually after pricing them. Enter tax and shipping separately in Half Life.

3D printing applies to enclosures and supports. Electronics, connectors, fasteners and hinge pins must be real purchased or already-owned components. Some listed printed supports still need CAD refinement.
