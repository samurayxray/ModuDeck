## Research findings

- Adafruit panel USB trackpads 5061 ($39.95) and 5062 ($49.95) are no longer stocked: do not use them as available purchase items. Sources: https://www.adafruit.com/product/5061 and https://www.adafruit.com/product/5062
- Adafruit 922 keyboard with integrated touchpad is listed at $49.95 and available, but is an alternative layout and does not implement the separate flip-up touchpad: https://www.adafruit.com/product/922
- Main display and cellular hardware still need verified quotes. The keyboard candidate needs enclosure changes. The Pi4 supply does not establish sufficient power for the entire system or display.
- Import from repo has not been tested. Missing vendor fields may prevent import; this draft is not ready for a funding request.
