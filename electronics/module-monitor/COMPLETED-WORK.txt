The existing ModuDeck Module Monitor schematic was completed and a 90 x 70 mm two-layer PCB was routed for an original Raspberry Pi Pico on two removable 20-pin female sockets. KiCad ERC and DRC, including schematic parity, passed with zero reported violations. PDF, Gerber, Excellon drill files, component BOMs and six renders from the actual design were exported, including a 3D cover. MicroPython debounce, active-low inputs, LED mapping and JSON reporting were checked in host simulations. No PCB was fabricated or assembled, no Pico hardware or real USB serial connection was tested, and enclosure fit remains unverified. Prices were checked on public vendor pages; unverified costs and shipping are marked for verification.
